AI-POWERED ROAD HAZARD & RISK INTELLIGENCE

See the Road Before It Becomes a Risk.

RoadGuard AI combines real-time computer vision, multi-frame object tracking, and geospatial intelligence to detect potholes, cracks, manholes, and surface failures—estimating severity and prioritizing municipal maintenance before catastrophic damage occurs.

🖼️

Computer Vision Detection

Classifies 15 road defect categories including potholes, speed bumps, fissures, debris, and waterlogging with sub-pixel bounding box localization.

🎯

Deterministic Risk Engine

Calculates explainable 0–100 risk ratings combining defect morphology, wheel track trajectory, water presence, and citizen report volume.

🎥

Temporal Video Tracking

SORT-based multi-frame IoU tracking prevents duplicate hazard count inflation across consecutive dashcam video frames.

🗺️

Geospatial Hotspots

Spatial clustering computes road hazard density per kilometer, identifying critical municipal maintenance priority corridors.

👷

Field Repair Lifecycle

Complete field inspector workflow from dispatch to repair completion with interactive Before/After evidence verification.

🤖

AI Inspector Assistant

Natural language query assistant grounded in database tools providing real-time smart-city road condition analytics.
